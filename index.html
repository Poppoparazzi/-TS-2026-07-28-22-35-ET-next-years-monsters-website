The evidence framework

Fast enough for a first look, deep enough for serious study. A rare compromise where nobody had to sacrifice the useful part.

Monster Rating™

A 0–100 educational evidence score that summarizes setup quality, fundamentals, trend, catalysts, and risk.

Monster DNA™

The durable traits behind the rating, including growth, margins, cash flow, leadership, and execution.

Market Weather™

The surrounding sector and macro conditions that can strengthen or weaken the setup.

Tipping Point™

The period when the evidence changed and the stock began behaving differently.

VCL™

Visual Case Library pages showing dates, prices, pullbacks, breakouts, and what happened next.

Move Driver™

Separates company-specific strength from sector, macro, and sympathy-driven movement.

Educational disclaimer: Monster Rating™ measures evidence strength. It is not a buy, sell, or hold recommendation and is not financial advice. Investing involves risk, including loss of principal. The screen does not find guaranteed winners. It finds evidence.
