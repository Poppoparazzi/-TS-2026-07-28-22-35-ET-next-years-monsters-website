# Public Page Finish Rules

A public Next Year’s Monsters™ page may be marked complete only when its visible promises work now, its limitations are labeled plainly, its links and mobile layout pass, its failure states are useful, and its automated checks pass.

Empty future sections, obsolete rollout milestones, prototype language, stale stock counts, and “coming soon” marketing are not completion. A capability that depends on an unconnected licensed provider or unapproved scoring engine must be removed from public promotion or replaced with an honest working alternative until it exists.
