The method

Three questions. Three answers.

A good company, a future Monster candidate, and a Top 15 research case are not the same thing. The system keeps those questions separate so a mature leader is not penalized for already being mature, and an exciting young company is not mistaken for a proven business.

1. Identify their stock

Monster Check™ begins with the exact ticker and official company identity. SEC evidence is used where available. If the company cannot be verified, the system does not continue by guessing.

2. Current Stock Rating™

This is the present-day question: how strong is the company now? The planned verified input set includes business growth, earnings and cash flow, margins, balance-sheet strength, market and relative-strength evidence, risks, and data freshness. Until those required inputs are complete, the public result says Data Incomplete / Not Yet Rated.

3. Future Monster Fingerprint Rating™

This is a different question. Active candidates are compared with the evidence visible in historical VCL™ leaders before their major climbs. The score is a research ranking, not a probability of future success and not a substitute for the Current Stock Rating™.

4. Monster Hunt™ status

Detective Break™ organizes active cases into Top 15, Rising Monsters™, Monster Watchlist™, and Dropped Cases™. A mature VCL™ leader can be labeled Established Leader instead of being forced into an emerging-company competition where it no longer belongs.

5. Read the evidence

Monster DNA™, growth, margins, demand, catalysts, relative strength, balance-sheet resilience, management execution and explicit risk deductions explain why a Future Monster case is strong or weak. Missing evidence remains visible as missing.

6. Open the VCL™ case

The Visual Case Library™ is historical education. Its case-study scores describe the archived example and must not be confused with a present-day Current Stock Rating™ or an active Monster Hunt™ score.

Scoring discipline

No decorative numbers.

A rating only deserves to appear when the inputs required by that rating are verified and sufficiently fresh. SEC identity alone does not create a Current Stock Rating™. A historical case-study score does not become a live score because the ticker is famous. And a strong Future Monster Fingerprint Rating™ does not mean a stock is guaranteed to rise.
