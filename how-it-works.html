The method

How Monster Check™ works

The first screen gives the rating. The next screens explain the evidence instead of demanding blind faith from a colorful number.

Enter the ticker

The system identifies the company and retrieves the data required for the rating.

Build Monster DNA™

Growth, margins, earnings, cash flow, relative strength, accumulation, catalysts, and risk are organized into a readable evidence profile.

Read Market Weather™

The stock is evaluated within its sector, peer group, rates, macro conditions, and current leadership themes.

Find the Tipping Point™

The system looks for the point where the business evidence or price behavior materially changed.

Assign Monster Rating™

The evidence is summarized into a 0–100 educational score and tier, accompanied by reasons and warnings.

Open the VCL™ case

Historical examples teach how similar evidence looked before past leaders became obvious.

Educational disclaimer: Monster Rating™ measures evidence strength. It is not a buy, sell, or hold recommendation and is not financial advice. Investing involves risk, including loss of principal. The screen does not find guaranteed winners. It finds evidence.
