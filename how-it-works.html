The current method

Start with evidence, then label what is actually available

Monster Check™ can identify a covered company, show official SEC evidence, and route visitors to clearly separated historical demonstrations and external market tools. It does not manufacture a live Monster Rating™ when production scoring is unavailable.

What happens now

A truthful path from ticker to evidence

1. Enter an exact ticker

Monster Check™ validates the ticker and looks for a matching company in the public production universe. Blank, malformed, or unmatched searches are not silently converted into a different company.

2. Confirm the SEC identity

When the company is matched to the SEC, the page labels the result Official SEC Evidence and provides source links. A company that cannot be confidently matched is labeled Unresolved SEC Identity.

3. Keep external tools separate

Charts, quotes, and news supplied by outside services are labeled External Market Data · May Be Delayed. They are not presented as SEC evidence, verified internal news, or a production Monster Rating™.

4. Use historical cases as demonstrations

The 15 book companies are educational VCL™ examples. Their printed historical scores are labeled Demonstration Rating, not current ratings and not a claim that the same conditions exist today.

5. Show the absence of a rating honestly

When production scoring has not supplied a current Monster Rating™, the company is labeled Not Yet Rated. No score, tier, history, or reason is invented to fill the space.

6. Fail visibly instead of pretending

When an API, market-data source, or external widget cannot be reached, the page uses Provider Not Connected and offers a useful route where possible. Missing data is not replaced with zeroes, stale claims, or decorative confidence.

Status language

What each public label means

Official SEC Evidence

Company identity, filings, and facts obtained through the SEC-connected production service.

External Market Data · May Be Delayed

Third-party charts, quotes, or stories whose availability, timing, and content are controlled by the outside provider.

Demonstration Rating

A historical educational score from an approved VCL™ book example. It is not a current production rating.

Not Yet Rated

The company may have SEC evidence, but no current production Monster Rating™ has been confirmed.

Unresolved SEC Identity

The production universe does not yet have a confident SEC identity match for the ticker.

Provider Not Connected

The required API, market-data provider, or external widget is unavailable or returned an unusable response.

Evidence framework

They are a framework for examining growth, financial quality, price behavior, catalysts, leadership, and risk. Their presence on the site does not mean an automated production score has been calculated.
