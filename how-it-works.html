02 / FOUR DIFFERENT THINGS

DO NOT MIX
THE SOURCES.

The framework is useful only when visitors can tell where each fact came from and what it can support.

OFFICIAL

SEC EVIDENCE

Company identity, filings, and standardized company facts obtained through the SEC-first backend.

EXTERNAL

MARKET CONTEXT

Charts, quotes, market heatmaps, and stories supplied by outside tools and subject to provider availability or delay.

EDUCATIONAL

VCL™ DEMONSTRATIONS

Book-based historical examples that explain the framework. They are not current recommendations or verified production ratings.

PRODUCTION

VERSIONED RATINGS

A future dated calculation that must use approved inputs, an explicit formula version, stored evidence, and reproducible history.

Unresolved SEC IdentityThe official SEC company mapping could not be confirmed for the requested ticker.
Provider Not ConnectedThe required service is unavailable or not configured, so no result is being claimed.

04 / THE PRODUCTION STANDARD

A NUMBER IS
NOT ENOUGH.

A production Monster Rating™ must be explainable and repeatable. It needs dated source inputs, an approved Version 1 formula, component evidence, warnings, a calculation timestamp, and a stored historical record.

Until those requirements are connected and verified, the website uses honest substitutes: official SEC evidence, external market context, and clearly labeled VCL™ demonstrations.
