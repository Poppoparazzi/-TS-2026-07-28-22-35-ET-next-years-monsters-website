# Monster DNA™ Version 2 — Prospective Validation Standard

<!-- TS: 2026-10-01 07:15 ET -->

## Purpose

Monster DNA™ Version 2 is a forward-looking research framework for finding companies that resemble historical pre-breakout Monsters **and** have a favorable current setup. It is deliberately different from the original August 8, 2026 Monster Hunt fingerprint score.

The original August 8 scores are frozen historical records. They must never be rewritten after seeing later returns.

Version 2 is designed from two directions:

1. What the historical winners had before their major advances.
2. What similar-looking non-winners were missing, or what negative traits they carried.

This is an evidence-ranking system, not a promise, probability forecast, or investment recommendation.

## The problem Version 2 is fixing

Version 1 could award a very high score to an exceptional business even when expectations, valuation, earnings freshness, or market confirmation were unfavorable. CRDO is the clearest stress-test case: the August 8 research board gave it a 94 fingerprint score, yet its later stock performance was poor over the first measured review window.

Version 2 therefore separates "great company" from "good setup now."

## Three-pillar score

The pre-penalty score is 100 points.

### Pillar A — Business DNA: 40 points

- Revenue growth and acceleration: 8
- Earnings / free-cash-flow inflection: 7
- Margin and scalability trajectory: 6
- Product/service demand, adoption, backlog, or customer growth: 7
- Competitive advantage / differentiation: 5
- Balance-sheet resilience: 4
- Capital efficiency / cash conversion: 3

Question answered: **Is the underlying business becoming materially stronger?**

### Pillar B — Inflection & Expectations: 35 points

- Latest-quarter acceleration versus prior trend: 8
- Management guidance direction and magnitude: 8
- Consensus estimate revision direction: 6
- Valuation versus growth and expected growth: 6
- Earnings-surprise quality, including margins and cash flow: 4
- Expectations gap: evidence improving faster than the market had already priced: 3

Question answered: **Is the business improving faster than expectations?**

### Pillar C — Market Confirmation: 25 points

- 3-month relative strength versus SPY: 6
- 6-month relative strength versus SPY: 5
- 12-month relative strength versus SPY: 3
- Price/volume accumulation: 5
- Drawdown, base quality, and distance from an exhausted move: 3
- Sector / market regime confirmation: 3

Question answered: **Is the market beginning to confirm the business inflection rather than reject it?**

## Anti-DNA penalties

A company can lose up to 30 points after the three pillars are scored.

- Material revenue deceleration: up to -8
- Gross-margin or operating-margin deterioration: up to -6
- Guidance cut or material guide-down: up to -8
- Broad estimate cuts: up to -6
- Extreme valuation without matching estimate upgrades: up to -6
- Material dilution / financing dependency: up to -5
- Dangerous customer concentration: up to -4
- Confirmed price/relative-strength trend break: up to -6
- Deteriorating cash conversion: up to -5

Penalties must be supported by dated evidence. A missing datapoint is not silently treated as favorable.

## Hard gates

A weighted average is not allowed to hide a fatal weakness. To qualify as an **Active Monster™ candidate**, all of these must pass:

- Adjusted Monster DNA V2 score >= 80
- Business DNA >= 70 / 100
- Inflection & Expectations >= 65 / 100
- Market Confirmation >= 55 / 100
- Anti-DNA penalty <= 15
- Latest earnings/guidance evidence is current
- Valuation/expectations evidence is current
- Market data is current
- No unresolved critical evidence conflict

A stock with a high Business DNA score but a failed expectations or market gate is **Watchlist**, not Active Monster.

## Status bands

- ACTIVE MONSTER: adjusted score >= 80 and every hard gate passes
- RISING MONSTER: adjusted score >= 72 and no critical gate fails, but at least one Active gate is not yet met
- WATCHLIST: promising DNA with one or more material setup weaknesses
- DROPPED / REJECTED: deterioration or Anti-DNA evidence invalidates the case

## Forward-test rules

The next cohort must be frozen **before** measuring future performance.

For each candidate save:

- ticker
- frozen score
- all three pillar scores
- Anti-DNA penalty
- evidence date
- entry/reference closing price
- SPY reference closing price
- 60-day closing price
- 90-day closing price
- 60-day stock return and excess return versus SPY
- 90-day stock return and excess return versus SPY

### Primary 90-day hit definition

A candidate counts as a primary hit only when:

1. its 90-day total return is positive, **and**
2. it outperforms SPY over the same interval.

This prevents a strong bull market from making a weak model look clever.

### Validation target

The research target is **at least 75% primary hits**, not a guaranteed result.

For a 15-stock cohort, that means at least 12 primary hits out of 15.

One cohort is not enough to claim reliable predictive power. Do not describe Version 2 as validated until it has completed at least **three frozen prospective cohorts (minimum 45 candidates)** with no post-hoc rule changes inside a cohort.

The 60-day checkpoint is diagnostic only. Do not change the frozen 90-day scoring rules after seeing 60-day results.

## Anti-overfitting rule

Do not tune a threshold merely because it would have removed a known August 2026 loser or included a known winner. The August cohort is a diagnostic sample. Version 2 rules are frozen before the next cohort and judged prospectively.

## Historical baseline

The August 8, 2026 Monster Hunt scores remain preserved as Version 1 research history. In particular, CRDO remains recorded at 94. Its later decline is evidence used to improve the methodology, not permission to rewrite the old score.
