NEWS NEEDS
DISCIPLINE.

The future production system will not declare a headline positive merely because it sounds cheerful. Every item must be sourced, dated, connected to evidence, and compared with the stock’s actual response.

1 · VERIFY THE SOURCEPrefer official filings, company releases, and reputable reporting. Repetition is not verification.
2 · RECORD THE TIMECompare publication time with the price move instead of assigning causation after the fact.
3 · IDENTIFY THE DRIVERSeparate company news from sector, macro, rates, or sympathy movement.
4 · LABEL THE IMPACTStrengthens evidence, weakens evidence, adds a catalyst, adds a risk, or causes no rating change.
Educational notice: News can be incomplete, revised, misunderstood, already priced in, or overwhelmed by broader market conditions. Nothing on this page is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold a security.
