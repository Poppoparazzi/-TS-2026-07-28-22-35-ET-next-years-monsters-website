01 / WHY IT EXISTS

FROM BOOK
TO EVIDENCE.

The great winners of the past rarely became great in one instant. Before the headlines, companies often left evidence in growth, execution, market position, catalysts, and price behavior.

This project helps readers practice recognizing those footprints without confusing pattern recognition with prediction. The screen does not find guaranteed winners. It finds evidence.

03 / THE AUTHOR

DAVID A.
SNIVELY, SR.

David A. Snively, Sr. is the author and independent publisher of Next Year’s Monsters™: What Tomorrow’s Monsters Look Like Today.

The book is a field guide to historical pattern recognition, research discipline, and risk. It asks readers to investigate what improved, what weakened, what deserves more study, and what no longer belongs on the watchlist.

“The goal is not perfect prediction. It is earlier recognition supported by evidence.”

04 / THE FIELD-GUIDE MASCOT

MEET CAPTAIN
BREAKOUT™.

Captain Breakout™ represents disciplined preparation, visual evidence, and the willingness to examine a setup before the crowd makes the story feel obvious.

He is a guide to the framework, not a prediction machine. Evidence, source labels, risk, and independent judgment still do the real work.
