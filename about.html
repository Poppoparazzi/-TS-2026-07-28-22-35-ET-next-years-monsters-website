About the project

Built for Monster Hunters.

Next Year’s Monsters™ is an educational research system built to help people follow evidence, compare present-day companies with historical pre-breakout fingerprints, and keep current ratings separate from historical case studies.

Three questions, kept separate

Monster Check™ separates a company’s verified Current Stock Rating™ status, its Future Monster Fingerprint Rating™ when it is under active research, and its current Monster Hunt™ position.

History is evidence, not a promise

The VCL™ Library studies historical leaders before, during, and after major moves. Historical case-study scores are teaching tools, not present-day ratings and not guarantees that another company will repeat the same path.

Missing data stays missing

If the verified inputs required for a Current Stock Rating™ are incomplete, the result is Data Incomplete / Not Yet Rated. The system does not manufacture a number merely because a visitor would prefer one.

The operating principle: The screen does not find guaranteed winners. It finds evidence. Every score, tier, case file, and historical comparison should make clear what is known, what is missing, and what could weaken the case.
