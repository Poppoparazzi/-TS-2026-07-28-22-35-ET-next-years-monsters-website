About the project

Built for Monster Hunters.

Next Year’s Monsters™ is an educational research system designed to help investors recognize evidence before a company’s leadership becomes obvious.

Website first

The public website introduces the method, offers a limited Monster Check™, and organizes the VCL™ cases.

App second

Accounts, watchlists, score histories, alerts, Top 100 Monsters™, and deeper evidence screens belong in the application phase.

Evidence always

No guaranteed-winner language. No unexplained score. No pretending a demo rating is live data.
