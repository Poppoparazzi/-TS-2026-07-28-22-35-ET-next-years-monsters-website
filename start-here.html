HOW TO USE
THE SITE.

This is the quick guide for book readers, investors, friends, and anyone else who lands here and does not want to decode a website like it was recovered from an ancient trading desk.

THE SIMPLE VERSION

  1. Start on the homepage and use the ticker search.
  2. Open Monster Check™ to study one company.
  3. Use Top Monsters to see the current 15-stock demonstration leaderboard.
  4. Use Full Charts to study price action.
  5. Use Market Pulse and News Radar for outside context.
  6. Read every data label. Some items are live external tools, while Monster Ratings™ are still clearly labeled demonstrations until verified live scoring is fully connected.
RUN MONSTER CHECK™ GO HOME

1. HOME

The homepage is the front door. It explains the idea, shows Captain Breakout, and gives you the fastest search box.

Use it when you are lost or when you want to restart the site flow.

OPEN HOME

2. MONSTER CHECK™

This is the most important book-release page. Enter a ticker or click one of the 15 VCL™ quick picks.

For the original 15 VCL™ stocks, the page shows the Monster Rating™, tier, explanation, Monster DNA™, Tipping Point™, Market Weather™, Move Driver™, chart link, news/filings link, and educational disclaimer.

OPEN MONSTER CHECK

3. TOP MONSTERS

This page ranks the 15 book-based demonstration companies. It also shows the live-data readiness board.

If it says “not live yet,” believe it. That board is honest status tracking, not a failure to read your mind.

OPEN TOP MONSTERS

4. FULL CHARTS

Use Full Charts when you want a larger external chart for one stock or a side-by-side comparison.
