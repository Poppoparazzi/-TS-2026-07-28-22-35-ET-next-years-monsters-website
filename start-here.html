WHAT EVERY DATA LABEL MEANS

LabelPlain-English meaningWhat it does not mean
Official SEC evidenceCompany identity, filings, or company facts were retrieved from SEC-connected services.It is not a stock recommendation or proof that the company will perform well.
External market data · may be delayedA third-party chart or ticker tool is providing market context under its own timing rules.It is not guaranteed real-time execution data.
Demonstration ratingA book-release or Visual Case Library example explains the Monster Rating™ framework.It is not a freshly calculated production rating.
Not yet ratedThe company is known or searchable, but the production scoring requirements are not complete.It is not a zero rating, rejection, or negative opinion.
Unresolved SEC identityThe automated SEC pipeline could not complete the expected identity/evidence record.It is not a conclusion about investment quality.
Provider not connectedA licensed quote, news, or other required production data source is not configured.It is not permission to substitute made-up data.
