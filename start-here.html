WHAT EVERY DATA LABEL MEANS

LabelPlain-English meaningWhat it does not mean
Official SEC EvidenceCompany identity, filings, or company facts came from SEC-connected services.It is not a recommendation or a production rating.
External Market Data · May Be DelayedAn outside chart, quote, or story provider supplies market context under its own timing and availability rules.It is not guaranteed real-time data or Official SEC Evidence.
Demonstration RatingAn approved VCL™ book example illustrates the Monster Rating™ framework.It is not a newly calculated production score.
Not Yet RatedThe company is known or searchable, but no verified production Monster Rating™ is available.It is not a zero, rejection, or negative opinion.
Unresolved SEC IdentityThe automated SEC pipeline could not complete the expected company identity record.It is not a conclusion about investment quality.
Provider Not ConnectedA required API or outside provider did not supply usable data.It is not permission to substitute guessed values.

WHAT WORKS NOW

  • Public company search: available through Coverage and exact-ticker routes.

  • Demonstration Ratings: available for the 15 approved VCL™ book cases.
  • Production Monster Ratings™: shown only when verified production output exists; otherwise the correct label is Not Yet Rated.
