Next Year’s Monsters™ now separates three different questions that used to be too easy to mix together: how a stock rates today, whether it resembles a historical pre-breakout Monster, and where it sits in the active Monster Hunt™.

THE SIMPLE VERSION

  1. Enter a stock in Monster Check™.
  2. Read the Current Stock Rating™ status. If required verified inputs are missing, it says Data Incomplete / Not Yet Rated. We do not manufacture a number.
  3. Read the separate Future Monster Fingerprint Rating™ when the company is part of the research board.
  4. Read Monster Hunt Status to see whether it is Top 15, Rising, Watchlist, Dropped, an Established Leader, or Not Yet Evaluated.
  5. Open the case file, evidence, chart, and historical VCL™ comparisons for context.
